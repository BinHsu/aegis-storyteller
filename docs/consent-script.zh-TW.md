# Consent script — draft (Traditional Chinese)

Status: **draft, not reviewed.** Read aloud by the speaker; the recording is both the
consent record and the voice sample. Keep sentences short and natural so the sample
is representative of how the speaker talks.

The script must cover: what is recorded, what it is used for, where it is stored,
who uses it, that it can be deleted at any time, and that the speaker may stop.

---

我是＿＿＿＿，今天是＿＿＿＿年＿＿月＿＿日。

我知道這段錄音會被存在這支手機裡。
它會用來做出一個像我的聲音，讓孫子孫女可以聽我的聲音講故事。
這個聲音只存在這支手機上，不會上傳到網路。
只有我們家裡的人會用它。
我隨時都可以要求把我的聲音刪掉。

如果我不同意，我現在就可以停止錄音。
我同意。
